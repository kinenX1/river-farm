The Sidesof the River

Build your life on your side. Beat the other side.

How to play

Walk with the joystick. You can’t run, you’re a big farmer.

Chop your one tree for wood. When it’s gone, it’s gone: sell wood at the shop on the river island and buy what you need.

The shop has 6 random items and restocks every midnight.

Tap Build to put down a farm, fence, house, barn, stone wall, a new tree, or a bridge to the other land.

Rain is rare. When it comes, your crops and trees grow much faster.
